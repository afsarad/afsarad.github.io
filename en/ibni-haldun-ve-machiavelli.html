About the book

The second volume of Society, Power and Order reads Ibn Khaldun’s ideas on solidarity and the state alongside Machiavelli’s The Prince and Discourses. It revisits the relationships between social strength, political decisions, prosperity and institutions through contemporary questions while preserving their historical contexts.
