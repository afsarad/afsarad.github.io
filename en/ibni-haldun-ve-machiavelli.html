Ibn Khaldun and Machiavelli

The Force That Builds Society and the Reason That Preserves Power

Adem Afşar

Final working stage

About the Book

This study compares Ibn Khaldun's ideas about social solidarity, asabiyyah and the formation of states with Niccolò Machiavelli's views on political power, government and the preservation of power.

Through approaches developed by the two thinkers under different historical and social conditions, it questions the relationships between the formation of society and the continuity of political power.

Main Areas of Study

  • Umran and asabiyyah in Ibn Khaldun
  • Power and political government in Machiavelli
  • The founding and continuity of states
  • Social solidarity and political power
  • A conceptual comparison of the two thinkers
  • The effects of historical conditions on thought
