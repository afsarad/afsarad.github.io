Privacy and Visitor Statistics

This notice is for visitors to afsarad.github.io, where Adem Afşar publishes his work on society, history and civilization.

Site owner and contact

The site owner and publisher of its content is Adem Afşar. You may send requests concerning privacy, corrections to content and publication permissions to afsarad@gmail.com . Providing the address of the relevant page is sufficient for us to understand your request; please do not send unnecessary identification or sensitive information.

Visitor statistics

GoatCounter is used to assess which articles are visited and how the site is used. Technical information such as page address, visit time, referring source, browser and operating system may be processed. Additional statistics, such as approximate location at country level, depend on the service’s data-collection settings. This measurement does not prove that an article was actually read or understood.

According to GoatCounter’s explanation, visitor counting does not place cookies or local-storage records in the browser and does not retain IP addresses in its permanent statistics database. IP addresses and browser information may be processed in memory for up to eight hours to distinguish repeat visits. The service’s servers are in Finland and Germany. For details, see GoatCounter’s privacy notice .

Hosting service

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it records visitors’ IP addresses for service security. These records are managed by the hosting provider separately from the site owner’s visitor statistics. GitHub Pages data-collection information and GitHub’s privacy notice provide further details.

Email and external links

The site has no membership or visitor-comment form. If you send an email, your address and the information shared in your message may be used to respond to your request and conduct the relevant correspondence. Sending email is also subject to the data-processing terms of your email service and Gmail on the recipient’s side. Google’s privacy notice provides information about this service.

When you open social media, reference or other external links, the relevant service’s own terms apply. Share buttons may use your device’s sharing feature or open an external platform you select. The presence of these links does not mean this site stores information you publish on those platforms.

Cookies and advertising

As of the date this notice was updated, Google AdSense advertising code is not active on the site. Cookies are not used for GoatCounter visitor counting. Services visited through external links may use their own cookies.

If advertising is activated, this page will explain the providers used, the purposes of data processing, advertising cookies and preference options. Where necessary, a mechanism for obtaining visitors’ preferences will be established before advertisements are displayed. Publication of this page does not in itself mean the advertising service has been activated.

Your preferences and requests

You can block third-party statistics scripts through your browser’s content-blocking settings; you can still access the articles’ texts in that case. Questions about emails you have sent or information under the site owner’s control may be directed to the contact address above. It may not be possible to identify a particular person’s record in aggregate visitor statistics. Requests concerning hosting or other services’ own records are handled through the relevant provider’s application channels.

Updates

This notice is updated when the services used change. Last updated: 7 November 2026.
