About the series

How are societies founded, how do they change, and how do they create the institutions of living together? While examining historical thinkers’ concepts in their own contexts, this collection directs new questions towards contemporary social problems.

Publication note: the first book is complete. Other titles and descriptions form a work plan, not excerpts from published books. Covers are drafts and contain no actual ISBN or barcode.

Completed · selected reading published

Understanding Ibn Khaldun

From ancient civilizations to modern societies

The founding of societies, asabiyyah, solidarity, production and the formation of political order. A study that reads Ibn Khaldun’s thought in its historical context and relates it to today’s social questions.

Selected reading page

Work in progress

Ibn Khaldun and Machiavelli

The force that builds society, the reason that preserves power

A study comparing Ibn Khaldun’s and Machiavelli’s understandings of society, power and political realism. It aims to discuss similarities, differences and the question of possible intellectual influence between the two thinkers through historical evidence.

Planning stage

Understanding Montesquieu

The limits of power, the balance of institutions

A draft investigating the effects on individuals of rules, institutions and limits on political power. It asks whether justice is achieved solely by applying rules equally or also by considering their consequences.

Planning stage

Understanding Tocqueville

Democracy, equality and living together

A study intended to examine the relationship between democratic decision-making, majority influence and the needs of individuals in the minority. It focuses on the pluralistic conditions of living together.

Planning stage

Understanding Weber

Legitimacy, bureaucracy and the modern state

A book intended to investigate relationships of authority, rules, legitimacy and access to information in modern institutions. It discusses the difference between knowing a rule and having the resources to comply with it.

Provisional title · planning stage

The Future of Living Together

Responsibility, social balance and shared life

A study intended to bring together the series’ earlier questions to discuss original propositions on circles of responsibility, social benefit, the equal value of lives and a shared future. Its title and scope are provisional.
