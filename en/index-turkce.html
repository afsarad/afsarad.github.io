About the author

Understanding society, questioning its causes

Adem Afşar has completed undergraduate degrees in sociology, archaeology, history, and international foreign trade and logistics management. He pursued postgraduate study in sociology to the thesis stage.

He continues to research and write about societies’ historical development, the formation of political power, relationships between individuals and society, social responsibility and the conditions of living together.

His work seeks to evaluate historical events through not only their outcomes but also the social conditions, institutional structures and human behaviours that produced them.

Understanding a social phenomenon means questioning not only its outcome but also the conditions that produced it.

Unpublished research and conceptual work encompass migration and refugee policies; alignment between the will determining such policies and implementing institutions; human rights and public security; social power relations in relatively closed settlements; gender perceptions across generations; and social expectations of political stability.
