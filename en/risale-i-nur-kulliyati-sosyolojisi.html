Our purpose

This project offers a space for reading the works of Bediüzzaman Said Nursi to understand the individual, faith and social life. Our central purposes include aspiring to eternal life while making sense of life in this world, and contributing to the search to understand, strengthen and preserve faith.

To be a means of helping even one person save their faith is among the most precious aims of this work. This intention shapes the sincerity and responsibility of our relationship with readers. We value giving every reader room to ask questions, reflect and pursue meaning through their own free will.

Our attention to the inner life also extends to the responsibility of living together. We consider our readings on faith, eternity, compassion, brotherhood, solidarity and justice in relation to everyday human relationships.

Our approach to reading and inquiry

Keeping the Collection’s service to faith at the centre, we examine its approaches to the individual, religious community, belonging, education, responsibility and social life. We state our religious purpose openly and present our sociological interpretations with their sources, reasoning and limits.

We clearly distinguish the source text by Bediüzzaman Said Nursi, our sociological interpretation of it, and ideas developed by Adem Afşar. Inferences about a community’s lived experience are not treated as direct consequences of the text’s counsels; social practices require separate investigation.

We present quotations with the work and section identified. In our existing essays, source passages can be read within the page, and readers can tap words in the quotations to see brief explanations. We maintain the distinction between personal interpretation and the meaning of the source text.
