Understanding society and questioning its causes

Adem Afşar holds bachelor’s degrees in sociology, archaeology, history, and international trade and logistics management. He pursued a master’s degree in sociology through the thesis stage.

Adem Afşar describes his approach as interdisciplinary sociological analysis: sociology remains central, while history, archaeology, philosophy, political thought and social psychology inform his study of social relations and human behaviour.

The studies consider historical conditions, institutions and human behaviour together to understand how social phenomena emerge.
